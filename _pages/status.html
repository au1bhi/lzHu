About this monitor

Monitoring location: Alibaba Cloud, Shanghai. A node is marked available only after a successful connection to the HTTPS test target through its proxy.

Loading the latency measurement mode.

The server probes every two hours. Results expire after 2 hours and 5 minutes. Refreshing this page reads the latest saved results without triggering another probe. Your experience may vary by network, client, and destination.

History covers 84 two-hour intervals over the last 7 days. Each bar shows availability from the last actual probe in that interval. Availability is calculated from successful and failed node checks and does not represent an SLA. Gray means no valid record; striped gray indicates a monitor error. Select a bar for details.

Status labels follow programming contest verdicts: Accept means success; Partial means some nodes failed; Time Limit Exceed means a failed connection check, including timeouts or connection errors; Skipped means no valid record or expired results.

History accumulates from the time monitoring is enabled.
